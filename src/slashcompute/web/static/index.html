How contributing works

  • Runs in the background at the GPU share you choose, only while contributing is on.
  • Only predefined job types run. Nobody can send your Mac arbitrary code.
  • Every step is metered in FLOPs and spot-checked by other Macs. Disputed work earns nothing.
  • Stop whenever you like. The current step finishes, then your Mac is released.
